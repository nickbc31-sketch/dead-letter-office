## Locked Canon

The following names, terms, and concepts are locked. Do not rename, redesign, or alter without an explicit design decision documented in this file.

### World

- **City:** Veyr
- **Setting:** Post-Collapse enclosed city-state. Year: 2147.
- **Corporation:** Helix Meridian
- **Resistance:** The Quiet Choir
- **Department:** Post-Mortem Communications Authority (PMCA)
- **Network:** Soulprint Network — mandatory for housing, employment, and healthcare
- **Historical activist:** Saint Orra (executed 2130)
- **Key locations:** Administrative Core, Meridian Heights, Archive Quarter, East Transit, Lower Veyr, The Drowned Steps, The Undercity, Saint Orra Square

### Characters

- **Protagonist:** Mara Venn, 31, PMCA Review Clerk
- **Antagonist:** Director Calyx
- **Resistance contact:** Jun Vale, 29 — officially declared dead, active in maintenance systems
- **Mara's brother:** Elias Venn — officially declared dead, network engineer
- **Automated overseer:** The Audit Voice — no face, no body; abstract surveillance entity

### Core Concept

**One-sentence hook:** Process the final messages of the dead, then discover some of them are still alive.

### Action Verbs (canonical — do not rename)

- Approve
- Reject
- Censor
- Archive
- Flag Anomaly
- Illegal Forward

### Structure

- 8 chapters
- 24 cases (C01–C24)
- 8 platform levels (P01–P08)
- 3 endings: **Broadcast**, **Control**, **Erasure**
